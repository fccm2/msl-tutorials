xmgk - preprocessing - for's

using recursive for's

unfortunatly, pppo1 was not recursive

pppo5 is recursive, but it can only preprocess
one variable

pppo11 is recursive, and can preprocess
as many variables as you wish

$ man ./pppo5.1
$ ./pppo11.opt start-79d.xml
<msl>
<image>
<new w="10" h="10" rgb="0,0,0" />
<print put="A 1 E" />
<print put="A 1 F" />
<print put="A 1 Z" />
<print put="A 1 T" />
<print put="B 2 E" />
<print put="B 2 F" />
<print put="B 2 Z" />
<print put="B 2 T" />
<print put="C 3 E" />
<print put="C 3 F" />
<print put="C 3 Z" />
<print put="C 3 T" />
<print put="D 4 E" />
<print put="D 4 F" />
<print put="D 4 Z" />
<print put="D 4 T" />
</image>
</msl>

$ cat start-79d.xml
<msl>
  <image>
    <new w='10' h='10' rgb='0,0,0' />
    <for val='TM,TZ' of='A,1 B,2 C,3 D,4'>
      <for val='TM1' of='E F Z T'>
        <print put='%[TM] %[TZ] %[TM1]' />
      </for>
    </for>
  </image>
</msl>

<- / ->
